# TARIK Brand Usage Guide
**UGOP Platform — Hinabangan National High School, Samar (DepEd Region VIII)**

---

## 🏛️ 1. Brand Identity & Crest Emblem
- **Platform Name**: **UGOP** (branded as **TARIK** in mascot and crest assets).
- **Primary Crest**: Circular forest green badge (`#0F5A36`) with a double gold ring (`#D9A834`), laurel leaf motifs at the bottom, and Tarik holding a golden trophy.
- **Usage Rules**:
  - **Login / Onboarding / Splash / PDFs**: Use the full circular crest emblem at 120dp or larger (e.g. SF9 headers, official reports).
  - **Top App Bar & Navigation Drawer**: Use the circular Tarik head/emblem mark at 32–40dp.
  - **Data Tables**: Never place the mascot in dense admin/registrar data tables; reserve Tarik exclusively for empty states, onboarding, feature cards, and success toasts.

---

## 🗣️ 2. Tone of Voice & Localization
- **Greeting**: Always greet users with `"Maupay nga adlaw, [Name]!"`.
- **Success**: Confirm completed actions with `"Salamat!"`.
- **Language**: Short, encouraging plain English with light Waray/Filipino cultural touches. Never childish in registrar or administrator views.
